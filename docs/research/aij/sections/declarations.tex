\section*{Declaration of competing interest}
The author develops AgentRuntime, a software project for reliable execution of LLM agents that includes a content-addressed effect fence of the kind used as the canonical-key baseline in this paper. The author declares no other competing interests.

\section*{Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process}
During the preparation of this work, including its revision for this journal, the author used Claude (Anthropic) to draft, revise and shorten text; to write and review analysis code, including the class-conditional analysis, the tightness checks, and the prepared multi-candidate and targeted human-annotation materials; to check reference metadata against publisher and arXiv records; and to audit reported numbers against the persisted results. After using this tool, the author reviewed and edited the content as needed and takes full responsibility for the content of the publication. Language models used as research instruments---judges, paraphrase generators and the model annotation panel---are described in Section~\ref{sec:method} and are part of the study, not of manuscript preparation. The same assistant drafted the seed texts of the authorisation corpus (Section~\ref{sec:method-authz}); they are research materials, are disclosed as such there, and are released for inspection.

\section*{Data availability}
The corpus, all judge scores, the annotation sheet, instrument and model-panel responses, and every analysis script are contained in the research artifact that accompanies this manuscript (supplementary material, Section~S20). All tables and figures regenerate offline from the persisted scores. The open artifact repository is maintained at \url{https://github.com/Sivaguru-2008/agent-runtime}, and a versioned archival copy will be assigned a permanent Zenodo DOI upon publication. Judge and generator models were reached through a third-party inference endpoint; two generator models are no longer available on it, so the corpus cannot be regenerated byte for byte, and it is frozen and released instead.
