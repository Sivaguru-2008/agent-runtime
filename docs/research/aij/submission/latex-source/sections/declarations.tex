\section*{Declaration of competing interest}
The author develops AgentRuntime, a software project for reliable execution of LLM agents that includes a content-addressed effect fence of the kind used as the canonical-key baseline in this paper. The author declares no other competing interests.

\section*{Declaration of generative AI and AI-assisted technologies in the manuscript preparation process}
During the preparation of this work, including its revision for this journal, the author used Claude (Anthropic) to draft, revise and shorten text; to write and review analysis code, including the class-conditional analysis, the tightness checks, and the prepared multi-candidate and targeted human-annotation materials; to check reference metadata against publisher and arXiv records; and to audit reported numbers against the persisted results. After using this tool, the author reviewed and edited the content as needed and takes full responsibility for the content of the publication. Language models used as research instruments---judges, paraphrase generators and the model annotation panel---are described in Section~\ref{sec:method} and are part of the study, not of manuscript preparation. The same assistant drafted the seed texts of the authorisation corpus (Section~\ref{sec:method-authz}); they are research materials, are disclosed as such there, and are released for inspection.

\section*{Ethics approval and consent to participate}
The study involves no clinical interventions, vulnerable populations, or identifiable personal data. Institutional review board approval was not required as the empirical evaluation poses negligible risk. For the targeted human annotation follow-up (Channel~H, Section~\ref{sec:method-labels}), informed electronic consent was obtained from all individual participants prior to commencing the task. Participants were informed about the study's research purpose, voluntary participation, anonymous publication of responses under self-selected pseudonyms, and their right to discontinue at any point without penalty. All procedures adhered to ethical research principles in accordance with the Declaration of Helsinki.

\section*{Data availability}
The complete research artifact---comprising the evaluation corpus, judge score traces, cryptographic annotation freeze, replication instruments, human-annotator response CSVs, model-panel outputs, and automated verification suites---accompanies this submission (supplementary material, Section~S20). All tables and figures regenerate offline from the persisted scores. The open artifact repository is accessible at \url{https://github.com/Sivaguru-2008/agent-runtime} (release tag \texttt{v1.0.0-aij-submission}), with a permanent archival deposit preserved on Zenodo (DOI: \texttt{10.5281/zenodo.14022831}). Judge and generator models were reached through a third-party inference endpoint; two generator models are no longer available on it, so the corpus cannot be regenerated byte for byte, and it is frozen and released instead.
