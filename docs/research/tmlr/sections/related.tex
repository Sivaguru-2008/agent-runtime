\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Identity by position, token and content}
At-most-once and exactly-once processing are classical systems problems, from remote procedure calls \citep{birrell1984rpc} to stream processing \citep{carbone2017flink} and sagas \citep{garcia1987sagas}. Durable-execution engines replay a recorded history \citep{temporal2026docs,restate2026docs,resume2026contract}, payment interfaces de-duplicate by client-chosen keys \citep{stripe2026idempotency}, and fencing tokens are standard practice \citep{kleppmann2017ddia}. All assume a retry is a faithful replay. Agent reliability runtimes make effects transactional or observable \citep{atomix2026,lyu2026coagent,balakrishnan2026logact,mozafari2026transaction} or verify postconditions before retrying \citep{mansoor2026verified}; they take the identity of an effect as given or rely on an observable postcondition, which, where it exists, is stronger evidence than any judge score.

\paragraph{Semantic replay}
ACRFence \citep{zheng2026acrfence} shows that agents re-synthesise subtly different requests after checkpoint--restore and proposes an analyser LLM to classify each post-restore call; the mitigation is not implemented and its misclassification is left open. CapLease \citep{xu2026caplease} names semantic replay in authorisation and binds a confirmation to a canonical action. We add a procedure with a stated bound on the safety-critical error, robustness to injected text on declared fields, and a measured cost.

\paragraph{Duplicate detection and semantic caching}
Record linkage \citep{elmagarmid2007duplicate}, semantic similarity \citep{cer2017sts}, LLM-assisted deduplication \citep{fang2026semhash} and semantic caches \citep{gill2025meancache} make similar decisions with cheap, roughly symmetric errors; here one error is irreversible and silent, so we bound one direction rather than report accuracy.

\paragraph{LLM judges and prompt injection}
LLM judges are biased, contaminated and self-preferring \citep{zheng2023judge,wang2023fair,ye2024justice,chen2024humans,li2025leakage}, persuadable \citep{hwang2025persuasion}, and blind to some agent-action risks \citep{yuan2024rjudge,ruan2024toolemu}; indirect prompt injection steers agents through their context \citep{greshake2023injection,debenedetti2024agentdojo}. Design-pattern defences constrain what untrusted text can reach \citep{beurerkellner2025patterns}; the refuter is such a pattern, a decision injected text cannot influence.

\paragraph{Conformal prediction}
Split-conformal prediction gives distribution-free finite-sample guarantees under exchangeability \citep{vovk2005alrw,lei2018distribution,angelopoulos2023gentle}; Mondrian variants condition them on a partition \citep{vovk2005alrw}. Applications to language models cover generation, factuality, robot planning and judge scores \citep{quach2023clm,mohri2024factuality,ren2023knowno,sheng2025judgeconformal}, and risk control bounds expected losses \citep{angelopoulos2024crc,angelopoulos2021ltt}. Known failures include distribution shift \citep{ulmer2024nonexchangeable,lin2025domainshift} and minority-class under-coverage \citep{tursunbadalov2026minority}. The union bound behind our correction is classically sharp \citep{frechet1935,galambos1996bonferroni}, and Bonferroni merging is precise under arbitrary dependence \citep{vovk2020averaging,vovk2022admissible}. Our use is deliberately simple; what differs is the object---a control decision with an irreversible consequence, whose deployed statistic is a maximum over a variable candidate set and whose calibration population must match a jurisdiction that changes whenever the decidable layers change.
