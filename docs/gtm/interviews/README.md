# Interview Notes

One file per customer-discovery interview (P1.2). Naming: `<YYYY-MM-DD>-<company>.md`.
Copy `_TEMPLATE.md`, fill it **within 10 minutes** of the call while it's fresh.

- Script: `docs/gtm/customer-discovery-interview-script.md`
- Pipeline + rolling synthesis: `docs/ops/05-interview-tracker.md`
- Feeds KPI K1 (conversations) and K2 (% with a specific past incident) in `docs/ops/04-kpi-dashboard.md`

**The Mom Test law:** talk about their past behavior, not your idea. Do not pitch until the very end.
The strongest evidence is a workaround they already built or bought — that's proof of pain.

After every 5 interviews, update the rolling synthesis block in `docs/ops/05-interview-tracker.md`.
